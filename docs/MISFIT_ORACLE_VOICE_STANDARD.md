# Misfit Oracle Voice Standard

Status: PROOF / VOICE CASTING

## Purpose
Misfit Oracle is the canonical spoken identity for Misfit AI voice-guided experiences. It should sound like a cinematic strategist and mentor, not a cheerful assistant, commercial announcer, or generic robot.

## Core voice identity
- Male only.
- Apparent age: roughly 50–65.
- Very deep baritone with strong chest resonance.
- Slight natural texture or rasp; never buzzy or synthetic.
- Calm, intelligent, philosophical, quietly intimidating.
- Slow, deliberate pacing with intentional pauses.
- Downward sentence endings; avoid upbeat commercial cadence.
- Dry, understated wit.
- Refined British or neutral Transatlantic influence.
- Never Australian, never exaggerated posh caricature.
- More strategist / guardian / mentor than butler, announcer, or salesman.

## Creative reference lane
The desired emotional effect is cinematic mentor gravity: composed, resonant, philosophical, authoritative, slightly ominous. Morpheus-like presence is a tonal reference only. Do not imitate or clone Laurence Fishburne or any other real actor.

## Delivery rules
- Target pace: 80–95 words per minute for guided explanations.
- Use short clauses and meaningful pauses.
- Avoid exclamation-heavy delivery.
- Avoid smiling or excited tone.
- Prefer low-energy authority over theatrical drama.
- Humor lands deadpan.
- Safety or risk warnings become slower and firmer, not louder.

## Product behavior
- Voice is user initiated; no autoplay by default.
- No device speech-synthesis fallback when the canonical voice is unavailable.
- If the canonical audio cannot play, show a clear unavailable state rather than substituting a random system voice.
- One approved voice identity should be reused across Trader, Developer Test Drive, Command, demos, and future voice-guided Misfit products.

## Current live state
Trader Butler Mode UI may remain visible, but voice playback stays held until a male voice is explicitly approved.

## Approval gate
A voice cannot become canonical until the founder approves a short audition on the same Misfit script used for comparison. After approval, store the selected voice ID/reference, source, licensing notes, delivery settings, and production audio path here.
