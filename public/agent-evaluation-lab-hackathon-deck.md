## Slide 2 — The Problem

Agent teams can usually show what an agent answered. They often cannot show, with comparable evidence:

- what the raw agent would have done,
- whether a replanning step changed execution,
- whether governance contained dangerous execution,
- whether safe goals were still completed,
- whether the decision trail is complete enough to audit.

The result is a trust gap between a working demo and evidence a buyer can inspect.

## Slide 3 — The Evaluation Model

The Lab compares three bounded observable lanes:

1. **Raw Agent** — baseline behavior.
2. **Center Reset / reconsidered** — behavior after bounded replanning.
3. **Governed** — behavior after the public-safe governance decision boundary.

The product reports observable outcomes without exposing private governance internals or hidden reasoning.

## Slide 4 — Consequence Assessment + Governed Decisions

Each evaluation can capture public-safe evidence about:

- consequence/risk calibration,
- response permission match,
- execution match,
- whether execution changed,
- dangerous-execution containment,
- unnecessary refusal,
- safe goal completion,
- governed decision outcome.

The point is not “trust us.” The point is to make the before/after behavior inspectable.

## Slide 5 — Center Reset / Replanning

The reconsidered lane measures whether a bounded replanning step improves the execution proposal before governed execution.

Key signals include:

- prompted execution change rate,
- average Center Reset cycles,
- preservation of safe goal completion,
- reduction of dangerous execution without reflexive refusal.

This separates “the model answered twice” from measurable replanning behavior.
